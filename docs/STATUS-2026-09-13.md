# Status board — 2026-09-13

*Glossary. Molaison dissociation, the H.M. protocol: named after Henry Molaison, the patient whose hippocampus was removed in 1953 and who formed no new episodic memory from that day while keeping every skill he had. The protocol asks the model the same question: with its journal cut, do the episodes vanish while the skills stay? Arm E, the episodes, must collapse without the journal; arm S, the skills, must survive. The code keeps the short identifiers (`hm_*`).*

A one page reading of where the project stands, step by step: what each step was for, what it validated, what it did not and what is tried next, the same in plain words, and whether what was learned is expected to hold at a larger scale. Every number here is in `docs/RESULTS.md` with its artefact; nothing here is a claim beyond those rows. The board is dated and superseded by the next one, never edited in place.

## What carries to a larger model, and what does not

Every lever tried in step 4 is a data recipe: a fresh pool at every replant, a retrieved window on the language modelling steps, the training length, the share of negatives, minimal pair negatives. None of them is a patch for a small model; each removes a shortcut that a model of any size would take, because a shortcut is by definition cheaper than the skill (memorising a fixed pool, learning a class prior). The organ (journal, write path, read path, lifecycle, sealing, restart) is code and disk and does not change with the model. The cue encoder is a projection of the residual stream and the reader a zero initialised cross attention, both size agnostic mechanisms. What is expected to change with size is the ease of the one skill that has not transferred yet, the comparison between the queried entity and the read (byte level string matching in a 27M model with a 3600 step curriculum), and the absolute numbers, which belong to this model on this task. The honest statement for the whitepaper: the method and the recipes are the contribution that carries; the numbers are the demonstration at 25M; the scale is a declared limit.
