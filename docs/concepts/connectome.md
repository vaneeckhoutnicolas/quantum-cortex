# The Connectome — hemispheres, commissure, topology — living groundwork

**Status banner.** Written 2026-09-05 ahead of its wave (founder decision). Groundwork for **RES-10 (W4)**, zero claims; evidence arrives as dated amendments. This page carries the project's founding insight (D-log: Nicolas's inter-hemispheric framing) into testable structure.

## 1. Biology, the parts that compute

- **Two hemispheres, one bottleneck.** ~200 million axons in the corpus callosum connect hemispheres that each hold billions of neurons: inter-hemispheric bandwidth is *scarce by design*. Split-brain studies show the exchange carries **abstracted** information — the bottleneck forces compression.
- **Lateralization is real, caricatures are not.** Specialization by hemisphere exists (language networks lean left in most people) but as *gradients of connectivity*, not binary modules.
- **Small-world topology.** Cortical networks combine high local clustering with short global paths — neither all-to-all nor ring: clusters plus a few long shortcuts.
- **Rich-club hubs.** A minority of regions are densely interconnected among themselves and carry a disproportionate share of long-range traffic.
- **Wiring is expensive.** Axonal volume and energy budget shape the whole layout (Ramón y Cajal's economy): the brain minimizes wire subject to performance.
- **Individuals differ.** Connectivity profiles vary between people — the anatomical ground of the neurodiversity principle (transposed as a principle, never a clinical claim).

## 2. Transposition (the RES-10 mechanisms, structured)

- **Bottlenecked commissure [mapped → RES-10].** Zone groups exchange through a limited-capacity broadcast where outputs compete for access (Global Workspace, credited) — the width is a dial; compression at the exchange is the *point*, not a limitation.
- **Small-world as the target family [candidate — new precision].** The inter-zone graph is drawn from the small-world family (clustered + shortcuts) rather than dense or purely modular — the ablation compares families, not single graphs.
- **Rich-club experts [candidate — new precision].** A minority of hub zones get a larger wiring allowance and inter-hub links; ties naturally to heterogeneous expert capacity (Rev7.a — Broca).
- **The wiring budget [mapped].** Total connection capacity is declared and paid for; dedicated arcuate-style links are purchases, not defaults.
- **Connectivity profiles = cognitive styles [mapped → the ablation family].** Local-dense vs long-range-dense vs small-world variants at equal parameters, each measured into a per-capability profile — including on the continuity triad.

## 3. Tests (shapes frozen; thresholds at wave opening)

Connectome sweep at equal params → the capability-profile matrix (does long-range density trade systematicity against generalization, measurably?) · commissure-width sweep → abstraction quality vs latency · topology-family comparison (small-world vs dense vs modular) at equal wiring budget · rich-club on/off at equal totals · profile stability: does a wiring style keep its signature across seeds?
