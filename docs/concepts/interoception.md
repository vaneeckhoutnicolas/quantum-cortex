# Interoception — insula and anterior cingulate — living groundwork

**Status banner.** Written 2026-09-05 ahead of its wave (founder decision). Groundwork for **W3**, zero claims; evidence arrives as dated amendments.

## 1. Biology, the parts that compute

- **Insula — feeling the body's state.** The interoceptive cortex maps internal signals (heartbeat, breath, gut, effort) into a usable representation; subjective confidence and "gut feeling" ride on it. With the ACC it forms the **salience network** that decides what deserves processing.
- **ACC — conflict and cost.** The anterior cingulate fires on **conflict** (competing responses), on **errors** (error-related negativity), and — crucially — computes the **expected value of control**: whether spending more cognitive effort is worth it here.

## 2. Transposition

- **Interoception = own telemetry as input [candidate → C6 → C3, W3].** The model's internal signals — uncertainty/energy at the memory interface, budget state, trust context — are fed back **as input tokens**, not just logged. Consequence: confidence-aware generation ("I am unsure here" grounded in a real measured signal, not a stylistic tic). Calibration becomes measurable.
- **ACC conflict = one detector [mapped — ADR-003 ruling].** Conflict/anomaly detection has a single owner feeding trust scores (RES-4) and the stress channel (RES-9) — no parallel heuristics.
- **Expected value of control [candidate — new mechanism surfaced by this page].** The ACC's cost-of-effort computation becomes the **compute-allocation valuer**: per span, is deeper processing worth it? It sets RES-13's recursion depth, NOW-2's escalation threshold, and the homeostat's spend — one valuation, three consumers. The deliberation budget stops being uniform and becomes *earned where it pays*.

## 3. Tests (shapes frozen; thresholds at wave opening)

Calibration: ECE / reliability curves with telemetry-tokens on vs off, at equal params · confidence-conditioned behavior: does the model consult memory or escalate more when its measured uncertainty is high (and less when low)? · conflict-detector precision/recall on injected contradictions · effort valuation: quality-per-FLOP with ACC-style allocation vs uniform depth — the allocation must pay for itself on the ledger.
