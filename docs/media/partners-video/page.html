the bench
The provenance benchmark: measure a memory system of your own on the record's questions, with the record's reading and its frozen conditions, without the model and without the organ. Your numbers are yours; the record claims nothing from them.
Your model decodes and cites under the contract; the organ keeps, seals, marks and vetoes. The pair's numbers belong to the pair: the record's protocol measures them.
Measured on one model people use, through a prompt and no training: the episodic conditions hold, the skill arm fails; a window in front of a frozen model has a cost on everything else it does (the record, row 46).
