**Skeptic's clause (binding):** F1 is adopted only through its own ablation — NorMuon-class vs plain AdamW on our 30M control, equal everything, verdict in the ledger. An optimizer imported on reputation would violate "measure first, then decide".

**Legal posture (hardened 2026-08-07, same day):** copyright protects *expression* — the code text — not ideas or published algorithms; implementing a method from its paper is the clean path, and it is the **only** path for anything core (C1–C6 and the model's critical path, always written from scratch). Borrowed elements are additional and peripheral by rule; preference order: pip dependency > re-implementation from the paper > adaptation as a last resort (permissive licenses only, provenance header + NOTICE). This is engineering discipline, not legal advice — before any commercial move, run a legal audit in the spirit of Kern's `docs/legal-audit.md`.
