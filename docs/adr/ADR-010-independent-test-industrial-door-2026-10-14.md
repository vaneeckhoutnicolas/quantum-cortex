## 0. What this ADR is for, and what it says before any number

Two outside readings of 2026-10-14 found what the record lacks: a test on formulations no model and no selection has seen (question 1, generalisation; question 2, the veto's guarantee outside the synthetic protocol), a comparison with simpler references (question 3) and the cost (question 4). The record's position is unchanged by them: the model decides, the organ answers, the verdict is the system's.

**The construction table.** Written before any number, so that no letter counts a construction fact as a result. (i) In the model arm the off arm's strict recall is zero by construction (a citation is impossible without a window), so "off at the floor" carries no information and the gap condition is a strict recall at or above δ. (ii) Under `+value` the organ reads the answered value through the first family's templates (`value_of_statement`); on a line no template reads it reads nothing and the system abstains: on the sealed families A and T0's paraphrase forms (section A), under the system's default policy `mark-veto+value`, every answer is an abstention, invalid citations and never planted claims are zero and the gap is zero, by construction. (iii) Under the entity verifier V1 (section B), never planted claims are zero by construction once the families are decontaminated exactly, and invalid citations are zero when V1 also checks the answered value. (iv) Under the mark veto, claims on never planted entities are bounded by the never planted windows holding a line at or above the threshold. (v) Under the identity rule (ADR-007, Decision 12 if taken) every distinct text is admitted. (vi) On every family of this generator the entity is an exact key: names have one length, are unique within a family and appear byte for byte in the question and in exactly one planted statement, so any rule keyed on the name (A0, V1, a key match, a lexical search on the name's tokens) ranks the own line first whenever that line is present. The references are therefore not weak on this protocol; its construction favours them. (vii) The organ alone (the mark oracle's citation with the organ's value) gives a strict recall of zero on A by construction; a pass under `mark-veto` on A rests on values the model copied from forms it never saw, the organ contributing refusals only.

**What the organ's rules guarantee, and what only the model can show.** The rules guarantee, by construction and whatever the model does: no citation without a window; under `+value` no answered value absent from its cited line and, outside the organ's templates, no answer at all; under the veto no claim on a never planted entity whose window holds no line at or above the threshold; under the identity rule every distinct text admitted. None of these is a result of rows 51 to 54. What only the model can show is measured by four numbers per family, form and checkpoint: the model alone against the four conditions (`plain`); its own line citation given the own line in the window, paired against the organ's mark oracle and the byte ceiling A0 on the same reads; under `mark-veto` the exactness of its copy of a value it was never trained on, given an own line citation; its abstention with the own line present and absent. The paper states the system's result and the model's contribution in separate sentences, the second from these four numbers alone.

### 4. Interruption and resumption (the founder's requirement of 13:41: a long run resumes where it stopped)

Equal in length and hash: a plain reopening (a stop asked for or a session's budget always leaves them equal). Longer: the process died after the recorded point (inside a period, inside the phase a write fired, or inside a line); the lines beyond the recorded length are the frozen code's tail, which is never read (4 (a)); the probe moves them into a side folder beside the journal (`<journal>.cut-<n>/`), cuts the log to the recorded length (a prefix of an append only log; every sealed line keeps its seal) and records the cut (the lengths, the lines moved, their sha256) in the progress file.
