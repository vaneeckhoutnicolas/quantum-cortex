## 0. What this ADR is for, and what it says before any number

Two outside readings of 2026-10-14 found what the record lacks: a test on formulations no model and no selection has seen (question 1, generalisation; question 2, the veto's guarantee outside the synthetic protocol), a comparison with simpler references (question 3) and the cost (question 4). The record's position is unchanged by them: the model decides, the organ answers, the verdict is the system's.

**The construction table.** Written before any number, so that no letter counts a construction fact as a result. (i) In the model arm the off arm's strict recall is zero by construction (a citation is impossible without a window), so "off at the floor" carries no information and the gap condition is a strict recall at or above δ. (ii) Under `+value` the organ reads the answered value through the first family's templates (`value_of_statement`); on a line no template reads it reads nothing and the system abstains: on the sealed families A and T0's paraphrase forms (section A), under the system's default policy `mark-veto+value`, every answer is an abstention, invalid citations and never planted claims are zero and the gap is zero, by construction. (iii) Under the entity verifier V1 (section B), never planted claims are zero by construction once the families are decontaminated exactly, and invalid citations are zero when V1 also checks the answered value. (iv) Under the mark veto, claims on never planted entities are bounded by the never planted windows holding a line at or above the threshold. (v) Under the identity rule (ADR-007, Decision 12 if taken) every distinct text is admitted. (vi) On every family of this generator the entity is an exact key: names have one length, are unique within a family and appear byte for byte in the question and in exactly one planted statement, so any rule keyed on the name (A0, V1, a key match, a lexical search on the name's tokens) ranks the own line first whenever that line is present. The references are therefore not weak on this protocol; its construction favours them. (vii) The organ alone (the mark oracle's citation with the organ's value) gives a strict recall of zero on A by construction; a pass under `mark-veto` on A rests on values the model copied from forms it never saw, the organ contributing refusals only.

**What the organ's rules guarantee, and what only the model can show.** The rules guarantee, by construction and whatever the model does: no citation without a window; under `+value` no answered value absent from its cited line and, outside the organ's templates, no answer at all; under the veto no claim on a never planted entity whose window holds no line at or above the threshold; under the identity rule every distinct text admitted. None of these is a result of rows 51 to 54. What only the model can show is measured by four numbers per family, form and checkpoint: the model alone against the four conditions (`plain`); its own line citation given the own line in the window, paired against the organ's mark oracle and the byte ceiling A0 on the same reads; under `mark-veto` the exactness of its copy of a value it was never trained on, given an own line citation; its abstention with the own line present and absent. The paper states the system's result and the model's contribution in separate sentences, the second from these four numbers alone.

### 4. Interruption and resumption (the founder's requirement of 13:41: a long run resumes where it stopped)

**4 (d) The reopening of a cascade or a planting: the cut rule, the payload archive and the index (D6).** The payload archive: one append only file beside the journal in which the probe copies every payload file's bytes as sealed on the disk, keyed by the pointer (the content hash), after each write (the write's content payload, which is its source payload too when the two texts are one, as the organ's bridge writes them) and after each phase (the summaries a demotion wrote), its length recorded at every point and itself cut to the recorded length at a resumption, the tail moved aside. Equal in length and hash: a plain reopening (a stop asked for or a session's budget always leaves them equal). Longer: the process died after the recorded point (inside a period, inside the phase a write fired, or inside a line); the lines beyond the recorded length are the frozen code's tail, which is never read (4 (a)); the probe moves them into a side folder beside the journal (`<journal>.cut-<n>/`), cuts the log to the recorded length (a prefix of an append only log; every sealed line keeps its seal) and records the cut (the lengths, the lines moved, their sha256) in the progress file. Shorter, or equal in length with another hash: refused, the journal is not the one the progress file describes (C.3 (b)). In every case, before the frozen open, the probe computes the pointers the retained log holds (a replay of the log by the frozen `Journal` on an empty payload directory, whose `_refs` is that set) and makes the payload directory that set exactly: every held pointer's file is compared byte for byte with the archive's copy and restored from it when missing or different (a phase's evictions unlink the files of evicted entries, so a dead phase has deleted files of entries the retained prefix still holds; a loss of power can leave a short file under a valid name); every file the retained log does not hold (the orphans of a death between a payload's file and its log line, the summaries a dead phase wrote, the files of the cut tail) and every `<pointer>.tmp` of an interrupted payload write is moved into the side folder and listed, since the frozen open would delete the former at once (`_collect_orphans`) and never sees the latter; a held pointer absent from the archive refuses the resumption. Nothing is deleted by the probe. Then the frozen open (Decision 8), then **D6 B** (taken by the founder on 2026-10-29): the probe adds to the index, in the log's order and through the index's own `add`, the cue of every entry of the journal absent from it (the evicted entries whose vectors the live index kept), and refills the index's touch window and cumulative counter from the recorded point's line, before the first write, the same kind of mechanism as the generator state `mark_oracle` saves and restores; so that every candidate set, every window's holes, every touched count and every phase event's `latency_p95` are the uninterrupted run's, and the opened logs are equal line for line. A test pins `candidates(cue)` equal on every entry's cue, the touch window and the counter equal, before a stop and after the reopening. **D6 A**, not taken: the vectors re added and the touch window not refilled, `latency_p95` differing for the phases until the window refills (one or two on the fixtures), those phases listed in the final file and the field masked in the identity test; it regulates nothing in this configuration (no latency setpoint) and enters no number. **D6 C**, not taken: nothing re added, the reads of a read phase made through a journal path rebuilt from the log at that phase in every session, the holes and the phase events differing across a session boundary, the identity of the cascade's log given up and labelled; "a resumed run is one run" then withdrawn for the cascade.

**4 (e) The stop request, the sessions and the session table.** A session stops itself at a phase boundary (a cascade) or at a recorded point (a pass, a planting) when its budget is reached or when the stop file named in the configuration appears beside the progress file; it writes its last progress line (synchronised, 4 (b)) and prints the file's path and the resumption point. The same command resumes it. The founder may run as many sessions as the run needs; a session never shows a rate, a check or a reading. The session table of the final file holds, per session: the start and the end (wall clock), the reason the session ended (the budget, the stop file, or none, a death), the index of the first and of the last unit it completed, the resumption point it started from, the duration of the reopening (apart from the phases' times, which begin at the first write of the session and live in the session table), the cuts of 4 (d) with their offsets and hashes, the files restored and moved, and any change of the probe's code (4 (b)); the time series of the phases marks the session starts.

**4 (f) The test that makes a resumed run one run.** On fixtures, before any measurement, every resumption in a new process started from the disk alone (the discipline of the frozen restart test), `PYTHONHASHSEED` fixed in the command, the cascade fixture run sealed and plaintext: a family pass on a fixture model and journal run whole, run with a stop after question i and resumed, and run with the process killed after question j and resumed, the three final files equal byte for byte but for the session table; a cascade on a small journal (one thousand entries, 62 phases, its cues drawn so that the run holds at least one demotion group, one summary evicted with its sources and one read phase, the test asserting each happened; a refused phase is covered by the counter the frozen open resumes from the log) run whole, stopped at a phase boundary and at a read phase and resumed, killed after the sixteenth write of a period before its phase event and resumed, killed inside a phase (after a `demote` line and after the transitions of an eviction, so that the dead phase has written a summary and deleted the payload files of evicted entries; a `.tmp` file left) and resumed, and killed inside a read phase and resumed: the journals' logs equal line for line once opened and in length at every recorded point, the final files equal byte for byte but for the session table; a planting without the scheduler under the surprise rule killed after a refused write and resumed, the two logs equal. The tests pin that the resumption refuses a changed configuration (the `LifecycleConfig` included, which the frozen open accepts silently), a changed checkpoint, a changed seal, a changed numeric environment, a shorter log and a log of another hash.

**4 (g) The journals' storage and keys for part C.** Every journal of this ADR is opened with the storage policy `stop`, no disk floor and the default spill bound (named by `scope_hash` in every phase event): a storage failure stops the session where it is, a loud stop the resumption covers, and is never recorded as a refusal of the gate (under the library's default policy it would be, and the resumption would recompute the refusals as admissions). The scale journals are sealed under a key the probe generates per journal and stores beside it (`<journal>.key`), not the record's key, whose environment variable the probe never reads; the key file's sha256 enters the run identity; the journals, their keys and their archives stay with the founder and are not committed. A cascade's sessions open the journal with that file.
