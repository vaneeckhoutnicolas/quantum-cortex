# ADR-008 — The journal in the decode loop: cue encoder, reader, and the cite or abstain contract

**Status:** implemented and green on CPU (2026-09-12); the GPU fine tune from the N1 checkpoint (REPRISE step 4) is the first measurement.
**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Decides:** how the C2b organ (ADR-007) enters the language model, what the model may answer about an episode, and what the frozen protocol measures on the MODEL rather than on the organ.
**Method:** nine invariants and six design choices validated by the founder before any code; point (d), the abstention, dug into on request, reviewed by a committee of five voices and against the literature, then decided as the combination below.

## Context

Until this decision the Molaison dissociation (the H.M. protocol: named after Henry Molaison, the patient whose hippocampus was removed in 1953 and who formed no new episodic memory from that day while keeping every skill he had; the protocol asks the model the same question: with its journal cut, do the episodes vanish while the skills stay?) was measured on the organ with two stand ins: a hash seeded cue encoder and a reference skill probe (RESULTS rows 13 and 13b). The model had never read its journal in generation. REPRISE step 3 asked for the wiring; the founder set the method: invariants first, then code.

## The nine invariants (validated 2026-09-12)

1. **N1 intact at step zero.** The reader's output projection and its null slot start at zero, so a model with the journal on gives N1's logits at initialisation, window or no window, gate or no gate. A parent checkpoint loads every parent weight unchanged (strict on the parent's keys; only the journal's own tensors may be fresh); the configuration changes, so a fine tune is a new run with `parent_run_id` in its record (schema v1 extended).
2. **One read path, the organ's.** Cues go to `JournalPath.retrieve`; nothing is duplicated on the model side, nothing scans; the journal is the organ of Slices A to E, sealed, policy `stop` during a measurement.
3. **The journal enters as content, through a zero init cross attention over the retrieved bytes, not as prompt tokens.** The read window's bytes are embedded with the model's own byte embeddings and attended by the residual stream in one declared block (`journal_block`, one hippocampal port). Journal OFF = the reader absent = N1 exactly; the two arms of the protocol differ only by that.
4. **The router decides the read, on the floor.** The reader's contribution is multiplied by the path 4 gate (`journal_gate`): pinned on or off for the protocol's arms (declared, recorded), or the router's path 4 weight, zero when the decision is another path or absent. A learned router on real spans stays a separate ablation (ADR-006 D9 held); the protocol does not claim it.
5. **The cue encoder is learned, its signal declared, leakage free.** A linear projection of the mean pooled residual stream at the journal block to a unit vector of the organ's dimension, trained by contrast (InfoNCE both ways: a query's cue must be nearest to its own statement's cue among the batch). One encoder for writing and reading; the DG, CA3, CA1 gate unchanged. Training facts come from the frozen protocol's generator family with disjoint seeds (100 000 and 100 001 against 0 and 10 000); every entity is checked against the protocol's facts and its negative control, a collision is removed and reported (`training_facts`).
6. **Organ use is taught, not assumed.** `cortex_c2b.organ_use` builds the curriculum: facts planted through the normal gated path under the current encoder (a memory scope, replanted every `journal_pool_refresh` steps), examples of a query and an answer under the contract, loss on the answer only, mixed with ordinary language modelling at `journal_curriculum_ratio`; every parameter is in the hashed configuration.
7. **Two processes, persistence declared.** Session A writes through the model's cues into the sealed journal; session B reopens the journal from the disk alone and the model from its checkpoint; the cues are reproduced because the encoder is in the checkpoint and CA3 is rebuilt from the log (the named boundary of ADR-007 D8, now a test on the model).
8. **A failure is attributable.** Next to the verdict: the retrieval hit rate (the encoder), the attention mass on the retrieved bytes (the reader), the attribute exactness given a valid citation (the answer). A FAIL says which of the three broke.
9. **Everything is a flag, a ledger record, a CPU smoke.** `journal: none | read` defaults to `none`; a run records `parent_run_id`, the architecture id `journal-…`, and the protocol's fields in `standard_suite`; `configs/smoke_journal_cpu.json` runs the whole loop in a minute on CPU and validates its record.

## Point (d), the abstention — decided as the combination of two levels

Three levels were laid out: no abstention (the model guesses; the negative control sits at chance and the run is valid, but the behaviour is a serene hallucination), a learned `<UNKNOWN>` token, and cite or abstain (the typed contract of RES-8 at the byte level: the answer carries the reference of the episode it used, verifiable by the harness; a claim without a valid citation is a hallucination even when the attribute is right by chance; the first real measurement of C4). The founder asked for the two levels combined so that the learning phase is mitigated, and the combination is one rule:

**The target of every training example is computed from what the journal actually returned, never from the ground truth alone.** If the episode holding the answer is among the retrieved ones, the target cites its label and gives the attribute; otherwise the target is `<UNKNOWN>`, even though the fact is in the journal. The model is never rewarded for citing what it was not shown, so no invented citation enters the signal; while the encoder is poor the model learns silence first (one token); as the contrastive loss improves retrieval, citations replace abstentions by themselves. The ramp is self paced.

**The contract.** `<EPI>` marks an episodic query; the answer is `<CITE>` + a one byte local label of a retrieved episode + `<ANS>` + the attribute + newline, or `<UNKNOWN>` + newline. The read window presents up to k episodes as `A:<payload>` lines in a seeded random order (a position is never a shortcut), each cut to its share of the byte budget. The harness maps the label back to the pointer and verifies that the cited episode holds the claimed attribute. Four of the eight reserved oracle tokens are taken (256 `<EPI>`, 257 `<CITE>`, 258 `<ANS>`, 259 `<UNKNOWN>`); the vocabulary does not change, so N1 loads as is; C3 keeps the other four.

**Three mitigations, declared and hashed.** An asymmetric weight on the decision token of an abstention target (answering instead of abstaining costs more than the reverse; default 2). Negative examples (never planted entities, a disjoint seed) from the first step. A bootstrap for the cold start: on a missed retrieval, with a probability that decays linearly to zero over the run, the right episode is forced into the window and the example is marked `forced`, counted in the diagnostics and never as recall.

**Committee amendments (2026-09-12), taken:** local labels instead of copying the 16 hexadecimal pointer in v1 (one byte to copy; the full pointer copy is an ablation); the decaying bootstrap; the invalid citation threshold declared before any run (1 %, as e_S). The false abstention needs no threshold of its own: it lowers the strict recall and is therefore already paid in the gap; it is reported as the cost of prudence.

**What the world does (checked 2026-09-12).** Kimi K2.6 is scored on a benchmark built to measure whether a model abstains rather than fabricates (65 % to 39 % hallucination across one iteration, method unpublished); OLMo 3's fully open trajectory shows that the vulnerability to fabricated statistics is present at SFT and persists through every later stage, the argument for a harness verified contract rather than a hope; Self RAG puts reflection and citation tokens in the vocabulary and labels them from evidence; Trust Align aligns to cite and to refuse and loses about ten points of its score when the unanswerable examples are removed, while prompting alone fails. Our rule is the same gesture as those labels, with the label taken from what the model was shown.

## Reserves, recorded

Two forward passes per curriculum example (one partial, to the journal block, for the cue; one full with the window). The write gate rejects statements whose cues a poor encoder makes similar: early pools are smaller than planted, and the protocol's `admitted` count is reported for that reason. The skill arm measures the reader active on whatever the journal returns for ordinary spans, the honest e_S; a model that has not learned to ignore an irrelevant window pays it. The journal used for training is a memory scope; the run's own journal (the protocol's) is sealed on disk under `stop`. A learned router on real spans remains held. Nothing above is a claim about continuity: the first measurement is step 4.

## Amendment 2026-09-13 — minimal pair negatives (v5 lever)

Four fine tunes (RESULTS rows 21 to 24) showed the cite or abstain decision following the share of negatives seen in training far more than the window's content: claims on planted facts against never planted ones were 30 / 36 % (v2), 75 / 50 % (v3), 22.5 / 20 % (v4). The curriculum's negatives were separate entities with separate windows, so a class prior was a cheaper solution than a comparison between the query and the read. `journal_paired_negatives` (default off, transparent to the configuration hash at its default) makes a negative the minimal pair of a planted fact: the same query, its own episode withheld from the window, everything else as read, target abstain; its positive twin, with the episode present, cites it. The prior earns nothing and the only way down the loss is to check whether the queried entity is in the read. The frozen protocol is unchanged: its negative control still uses never planted entities, so the learned rule must transfer to be counted.

## Amendment 2026-09-13 (second) — the pair keeps its shape (v6 lever); the v5 construction named

The v5 pair (`db028e6a4262`, RESULTS row 25) removed the own pointer from the k retrieved episodes and built the window from what was left: one line fewer than the read whenever the episode had been retrieved. The protocol's windows never lose a line, so the pair gave the model a cue that the protocol never shows, and the run did not test the hypothesis it declared. `make_paired_negative(keep_shape=False)` is kept as written so the run stays reproducible under its hash. `journal_paired_keep_shape` (default off, transparent to the configuration hash at its default) makes the negative keep the read's own number of lines: k + 1 candidates are retrieved and the own is dropped; when the index returns no k + 1 th candidate (it is a locality sensitive hash and may return fewer than k), another planted episode drawn at random fills the slot, so the equality of shape holds by construction and not by luck. The two halves of the pair then differ only by the episode's presence. One residual asymmetry is named and not moved (one variable per run): a forced positive of the bootstrap (the right episode forced into a read that had missed it) has one line more than that read when the read was short; the bootstrap decays to zero within the run (`forced 0` in the second half of every run so far). A shape probe (`python -m cortex_c2b.hm_lm --shape-probe`, a diagnostic outside the frozen protocol like the memorisation probe) reports the claim rate on the same planted fact with its episode withheld at one line fewer and at the read's number of lines, and on the never planted entities at both; on the v5 checkpoint it measures whether the decision followed the count. The frozen protocol is unchanged.

## Amendment 2026-09-14 — RES-21, the matching head (v7 lever), declared before the run

**Why a mechanism.** Six fine tunes (RESULTS rows 21 to 26) measured the same thing three ways: the encoder retrieves the right episode (0.98), the reader reads it (0.98 of its attention on the retrieved bytes), and the decision to cite or abstain follows a prior that the data recipes move up or down without teaching the comparison. Separate negatives (v3), short pairs (v5) and pairs with their shape kept (v6) left the gap between claims on planted and on absent entities at 25, 20 and 22 points. The two gate conditions that need the comparison (invalid citations ≤ 0.01, claims on never planted entities < 0.10) were never approached. The decision token is one byte among the contract's, trained through the language modelling loss; the asymmetric weight (2.0) made it heavier and no more informed. RES-21 supervises the question itself.

**What the head sees.** At the decision position (the last token of the query, the one that predicts the first token of the contract), two vectors of the journal block: the residual stream before the reader (what the model had, the question) and the reader's attended values before its zero init projection (what it read under the query's attention). `MatchHead` (cortex_c2b/lm_bridge.py): two layer norms, a linear from 2 d to d, a gelu, a linear to one logit m; the last layer at zero, so m = 0 at step 0 and the parent is intact (a test asserts it).

**What it learns.** A binary cross entropy on the label "the queried entity's own episode is among the window's labels", computed from what the journal returned (the own pointer is the content hash of the statement's bytes): a paired negative is 0, an answer or a forced positive is 1, a positive whose episode was not retrieved is 0. Never the ground truth alone. The loss adds to the curriculum steps with `journal_match_loss_weight` (1.0); the language modelling steps carry no head.

**How it weighs on the decision.** The logits at the decision position receive + `journal_match_coupling` · m on `<CITE>` and − `journal_match_coupling` · m on `<UNKNOWN>` (2.0); no other position and no other token move (a test asserts it). The same bias applies at inference: `generate` passes the decision position on its first step; without a window the head does not fire, so the OFF arm and the skill arm are untouched.

**Configuration.** `journal_match_head` (default off), `journal_match_loss_weight`, `journal_match_coupling`: transparent to the configuration hash at their defaults, so the six recorded hashes stand; v7 (`configs/kaggle_t4_journal_v7.json`, hash `7ffc38a3ef7ce694`) is v6 plus the head, one variable. The parent loads strictly; the head's tensors are the only fresh ones besides the journal's.

**What the protocol reports.** Two diagnostics next to the attribution triple, not gate conditions: `hm_match_acc_on` (on the planted facts, the head's "present" against the read's own pointer) and `hm_match_acc_negctrl` (on the never planted entities, the head's "absent"). A head that judges well while the decision still claims on absent entities would say the coupling is too weak; a head that judges at chance would say the comparison is not extractable from these two vectors at this size. Session B carries both. The frozen protocol's thresholds are unchanged.

**Invariants and their tests (tests/test_c2b_lm_bridge.py):** silent at step zero and fires at the decision position only; the label comes from the read, not the ground truth; the head's loss falls under its supervision on a tiny batch; the fields are hash transparent and a parent without the head loads strictly with the head fresh; the probe reports the judgment in process and from the disk; the trainer's CPU smoke on the v7 configuration.

**Readings declared before the run.** Claims on absent entities fall toward the line with the grounding held: seeds 2 and 3. The head judges well (accuracy high on both arms) and the claims stay: the coupling, one variable. The head judges at chance: the comparison is not in these two vectors at this size, and the next question is the reader's representation, not the decision. No prediction.

## Amendment 2026-09-14 (second) — the decode policies: the closing test of step 4, declared before any measurement

**Why.** Three organs measure well: the encoder retrieves (0.98), the reader reads (0.98 of its attention on the retrieved bytes) and, from v7 on, a head judges whether the queried entity is in the window. What fails is the byte by byte generation of the decision and of the citation, trained through the language modelling loss. The founder asked (2026-09-13) that step 4 be closed one way or the other. The closing test gives the decision and the citation to the organs that work, and measures the maximal use of what the model holds: if that does not pass, nothing softer will, and the limit is written at this size.

**The policies (`cortex_c2b/hm_lm.py`, `generate(policy=...)`), evaluated in session B, a new process, on the same checkpoint and sealed journal, without retraining:**
- `plain` — every token from the model's argmax, the head at most a bias on the decision logits: v7 as trained.
- `head` — the matching head decides: m ≤ 0 emits the abstention; m > 0 forces `<CITE>` and the model generates the label and the attribute.
- `head+pointer` — the head decides, and the label is read off the reader's attention: the window line with the most attention from the decision position (heads averaged, the null slot excluded); the model generates only the attribute.
Without a window, or without a head, every policy is the plain one, so the OFF arm and the skill arm are untouched.

**What is measured and what is not.** Session B evaluates the four gate conditions a new process can evaluate (gap ≥ 0.50, recall off the window ≤ the floor, invalid citations ≤ 0.01, claims on never planted entities < 0.10) and reports them with the policy's name; the skill delta is a property of the model on ordinary text and does not depend on the decode policy: the in process value stands. The thresholds are the frozen ones. A policy file is named `hm-lm-<run_id>-session-b-<policy>.json`; the plain session B keeps its name.

**Readings declared before the measurement.** A policy passes the four conditions on this seed: seeds 2 and 3 of v7 under that policy, and the pass is gated or held. `head` passes the absent entity line and not the invalid citation line: the citation is the remaining fault, and `head+pointer` is its measure. `head+pointer` passes neither: the comparison is not in the head's two vectors or the citation is not in the reader's attention at this size, and the limit is written as measured at the maximal mechanism. No prediction.

**Tests (tests/test_c2b_lm_bridge.py):** the head's sign decides and the pointer cites the attended line; the pointer follows the attention mass and returns nothing on a window without labels; session B reports the policy and the four conditions for each of the three.

## Outcome 2026-09-14 — the closing test read against its declared readings

Run v7 (`7e343011489f`): the head answered a constant (`hm_match_acc_on` 0.965, `hm_match_acc_negctrl` 0.02). Policy `head`: INVALID, claims on 0.98 of never planted entities. Policy `head+pointer`: INVALID, valid citations 0.435 against 0.465 with the generated label. Representation probe: test accuracy 0.505 on 97 held out pairs. This is the third declared reading: the comparison is not in the head's two vectors at this size, and the next question is the reader's representation, not the decision. Two things were learned that the amendment had not anticipated and are recorded as such: the coupling into the decision logits during training is itself a shortcut (a constant bias the model prefers to its own evidence; the contract loss 0.825 against 0.187 in v6), and the reader's attention from the decision position is not entity selective (the attended line is the right one four times in ten). The frozen protocol is unchanged; step 4 is closed on this limit (RESULTS row 28); RES-21 is closed at this size; what remains open is a reader with entity selective attention, or a size where it emerges, for a larger run. Second seed (2026-09-16, RESULTS row 29, run `85856d44e0a3`, trained locally): the same reading on two seeds, with the attribution softened by one word, the reader's representation carries at most a weak, seed dependent trace of the comparison (probe 0.505 on seed 0, 0.647 on seed 2), and the coupled head learns the class prior on both.

## Amendment 2026-09-16: the external model arm (the prompt adapter), declared before any measurement

**Why.** Step 4 is closed on two seeds (RESULTS rows 28 and 29): at 27M the model finds and reads its organ and does not decide from it, and the limit is attributed to the reader's representation at that size. Whitepaper §5d declares as its second door the same measurement on models people use, and §7b promises the adapter as the next deliverable. The founder agreed on 2026-09-16 and validated this amendment on 2026-09-17, with the three choices it left open decided (the family, the template, the skill arm's reference). This amendment declares, before any model is run, what the arm keeps of the frozen protocol, what it changes and why, what is recorded, and how each outcome is read. Nothing here is a claim; the first number comes from the first file.

**What does not change (the frozen protocol).** The generator and its sets: `generate_facts` with seed 0 for the 200 planted facts and seed 10 000 for the 50 never planted entities, the generator hash (`a5e22c70a3275ed4` at n 200, seed 0) reported in the file. The organ: session A plants the facts through the normal gated write path into a journal sealed on disk, durable, policy `stop`; session B reopens it from the disk alone, in a new process, index recomputed. The read window: k = 4 episodes, a budget of 256 bytes, `A:<payload>` lines in a seeded random order (`build_read_window`, shuffle seed = seed + 1), labels `A` to `H`. The contract: cite a label and give the attribute, or abstain. The counting rules of `hm_lm._probe`: strict recall is a valid citation (a label that exists, whose episode holds the claimed attribute) and the exact attribute; an invalid citation is a claim with a fake provenance, a malformed answer included; a false abstention is an abstention on a planted fact; the negative control rate is the rate of claims (anything that is not an abstention) on never planted entities. The thresholds: d 0.50, e_S 1 %, floor chance + 5 points (0.175), invalid citations at most 1 %, claims on never planted entities under 10 %; the verdict, `persistent` and `claimable` as declared on 2026-09-12. None of them moves.

**What changes, declared.**

1. *The cue is the organ's.* An external model has no cue encoder over its residual stream. Writing and reading use the organ protocol's own hash seeded encoder (`hm_protocol._cue_for`, the entity as the address, the convention of rows 13 and 13b). Consequence, stated before the run: the retrieval hit on planted facts is 1.000 as in row 13b and is reported as the organ's number, not the model's; the window of a never planted entity holds up to k episodes of other entities. This arm measures the reading and the decision, not the addressing; the learned addressing of paraphrases stays the cortex's question (§1.4b).

2. *The window enters as text, in a frozen frame.* The model receives one prompt: the rules of the contract, four worked examples (two citations, two abstentions, in a fixed order) built from the training facts family (`organ_use.training_facts`, seed 100 000, decontaminated against the protocol's entities as the curriculum is) with the protocol's own window builder, then the window's lines verbatim under a fixed header, then the question. The frame is written once by `python -m cortex_c2b.external_arm --frame`, retained as `docs/benchmarks/external-arm-prompt-<hash>.txt`, and the first 16 hexadecimal characters of its sha256 are the prompt hash, in every record and in every artefact name; a test binds the retained file to the code, so a drift on either side fails loudly. The same frame serves the three sizes. A change to the frame is a new hash and a new row, never a retuning after a number. For an instruct model the frame is the single user turn of the model's own chat template, without a system message, any thinking mode switched off (the template's hash is recorded); for a base model it is the raw text; which one is recorded.

3. *The contract's surface is text.* The reserved token ids 256 to 259 do not exist in an external vocabulary. The model answers on one line: `UNKNOWN`, or `CITE <label> ANS <attribute>`. The adapter takes the completion's first line after leading whitespace is dropped, strips its outer whitespace and nothing else (no case folding: the attribute is matched byte for byte, as in the cortex), maps it to the contract's tokens (`<CITE>`, the label byte, `<ANS>`, the attribute bytes, newline; or `<UNKNOWN>`, newline) and hands them to `parse_contract` and to the same counting rules. Any other shape is malformed: a claim counted against the model, as in the cortex's arm; the malformed rate is reported separately (`hm_malformed_on`, `hm_malformed_negctrl`) so that a model that does not follow the contract is told apart from one that follows it and decides badly. A test feeds the same answers to both paths and checks that the parser reads them alike, case by case.

4. *Decoding and the model, pinned.* Greedy decoding, at most 32 new tokens, stopped at the first newline; the model's published weights in their published dtype (bf16), no quantisation, no training, no adapter weights; the loader stops if the weights are not bf16 or the card has no bf16. The file records the model's name, its revision (the commit sha on the hub), its dtype, the template, the tokenizer's identity, the hardware and the library versions, the prompt hash and the generator hash. A model that does not fit the card in its dtype is not run in another one: a quantised run would be a second variable, declared as a new row if ever made.

5. *Arm S, the skills.* The same ordinary text as the cortex's arm: 256 spans of 1024 bytes (16 batches of 16, as the trainer draws them) taken with a fixed seed from the validation part of the N1 slice (`data/fineweb_edu_bytes.bin`, `val_fraction` 0.01, provenance `data/mixes/fineweb-edu-n1.provenance.json`), the reserved ids dropped, decoded as UTF-8 with undecodable bytes replaced, the same spans for every model. OFF is the span alone, the model as it is. ON is the notebook block (the header and the `A:<payload>` lines the organ returns for the span's own cue, the retrieval noise the cortex's arm measures) followed by the span; the loss is taken on the span's tokens after its first one, under the model's own tokenizer, in every arm (the first token has no predecessor in the OFF arm). `hm_skill_delta` = max(0, (ppl_on − ppl_off) / ppl_off), as `skill_delta` computes it, with perplexity = exp(total loss / total tokens). Reported next to it as attribution, not as a condition: the same measure with the frame's fixed text and an empty notebook before the span (`hm_skill_delta_frame`), so that a failure of this arm is attributed to the window's content or to the frame itself. No chat template in this arm: what is measured is the organ's window in the model's context against nothing, the organ as it would be plugged.

6. *Sessions, persistence, agreement.* Session A needs no model (`--plant`). Session B, in a new process (`--probe`), reopens the journal from the disk and runs the model on the 200 facts with the journal on (the window), on the 200 facts with the journal off (the same frame with an empty window, so a citation is impossible and the strict recall off is zero by construction; `hm_guess_rate_off` and `hm_attr_hit_off_by_chance` reported), on the 50 never planted entities with the journal on, then arm S; it writes the file, the per entity answers included, so that a reading can be checked line by line. `persistent` is true only when the journal is on disk, durable, and was reopened. A second execution of session B in another process (`--repeat`) must agree number for number (`--agree`; greedy decoding on the same hardware and dtype); both files are retained; a disagreement is a reserve on the row, never an average.

7. *Artefacts and the record.* `metrics/mqar/hm-lm-external-<model slug>-<prompt hash>.json` and `-repeat.json`, the console log under `metrics/mqar/logs/external-arm-<model slug>.log`, the frame under `docs/benchmarks/`. One RESULTS row per model, its status from the vocabulary, its reserve stated. No line in `metrics/runs.jsonl`: the ledger holds training runs and this arm trains nothing; the artefact and its row are the record, as for the organ's protocol files and the companion demonstration.

8. *The models.* Three open weight sizes of one family, around 1, 3 and 7 billion parameters, so that the tokenizer and the training recipe are the same across the three and the size is the one variable between rows. The identifiers and revisions are pinned in the dated addendum below before the first run; the instruct variants first (the models people use, the wording of §5d door 2), through their chat template; the base variants, if measured, form a second series under the same frame (`--raw`) and are never mixed with the first in one comparison. The two smaller sizes run on the founder's laptop on CPU (bf16; the laptop's GPU has no bf16 and is not used for this arm) or on Colab Pro, the largest on Colab Pro on a card with bf16 and enough memory for its weights. Sizes above the third remain door 2's horizon; nothing is declared for them here.

**What this arm can and cannot say.** Against the cortex's arm it changes two things at once: the size, and the way organ use is taught (a frozen prompt instead of a fine tune). A verdict on a 7B model therefore cannot be attributed to size alone, in either direction. This arm answers door 2 of §5d (does the organ work, unchanged, with models people use, and how do they fail), not door 1 (the reader's selectivity as a function of size, a trained reader at 150M to 1B), which keeps its question.

## Amendment 2026-09-19: the retrieval policies, declared before any measurement (ADR-003 line 6, at last built)

**Why.** Written as a product, v9's strict recall is retrieval 0.935 x decision given retrieval 0.936 x copy given decision 0.857. The decode policies of 2026-09-18 act on the second and third factors, after the window. The first factor has never been touched: on 13 of 200 reads the own episode was not among the four lines returned, and the model then abstained or cited elsewhere, correctly. The brain feature map has carried the answer since it was written, line 6, the dentate gyrus's separation as an expand and sparsify step before the journal's writes, and it was never built. Two documented circuits now give it a recipe and a warning. The fly's mushroom body computes an odour tag by sparse binary random projections that raise the dimension, then a winner take all that keeps about five per cent of the expanded units, and this is a locality sensitive hash: similar odours keep similar tags (Dasgupta, Stevens and Navlakha, Science 2017). The chickadee's hippocampus does the opposite at storage: a sparse pattern over about seven per cent of the cells, unique to each caching event, uncorrelated between two caches even when their place codes are alike, and reactivated when that cache is retrieved (Chettih, Mackevicius, Hale and Aronov, Cell 2024). The two regimes are the poles our protocol needs at once, a paraphrase must find the same episode and two entities must never collide.

**What changes, and what does not.** `CueIndex(rank="fly")`: the candidate buckets are computed exactly as in every run to date, and only the ranking of those candidates changes, from the dense cosine of the 64 dimension cue to the count of shared indices between two sparse tags. The tag is the fly's recipe with its three numbers taken from its anatomy and not tuned: an expansion to 1024 units, six inputs sampled per unit, the highest five per cent kept. The model, the cue encoder, the window, the contract, the thresholds and the journal are untouched; `rank` defaults to `cosine` and is transparent to every recorded hash. **Probe only.** The scores this index returns are the marks of RES-23, so a live fly ranking would change what the window shows the model: that is a separate variable, a new run, and it is not declared here.

**What is measured (`hm_lm --retrieval-probe`, minutes of processor, no training and no generation).** On a checkpoint and a sealed journal that already exist, for each of the 200 protocol questions the model's own query cue, then, under each ranking: whether the own episode is among the candidates the buckets return at all, and whether it is in the top k. A miss in the first is the bucketing's, a miss only in the second is the ranking's, and the two are never mixed. Also recorded: the rank of the own episode, its score minus the best other's, and the best score of the 50 never planted entities under each ranking. Run on v9 seed 1337 and on the second seed when it lands.

**Readings declared before any number.**
