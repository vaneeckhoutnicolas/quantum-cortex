# ADR-008 — The journal in the decode loop: cue encoder, reader, and the cite or abstain contract

**Status:** implemented and green on CPU (2026-09-12); the GPU fine tune from the N1 checkpoint (REPRISE step 4) is the first measurement.
**Date:** 2026-09-12
**Decides:** how the C2b organ (ADR-007) enters the language model, what the model may answer about an episode, and what the frozen protocol measures on the MODEL rather than on the organ.
**Method:** nine invariants and six design choices validated by the founder before any code; point (d), the abstention, dug into on request, reviewed by a committee of five voices and against the literature, then decided as the combination below.

## Context

Until this decision the H.M. dissociation was measured on the organ with two stand ins: a hash seeded cue encoder and a reference skill probe (RESULTS rows 13 and 13b). The model had never read its journal in generation. REPRISE step 3 asked for the wiring; the founder set the method: invariants first, then code.

## The nine invariants (validated 2026-09-12)

1. **N1 intact at step zero.** The reader's output projection and its null slot start at zero, so a model with the journal on gives N1's logits at initialisation, window or no window, gate or no gate. A parent checkpoint loads every parent weight unchanged (strict on the parent's keys; only the journal's own tensors may be fresh); the configuration changes, so a fine tune is a new run with `parent_run_id` in its record (schema v1 extended).
2. **One read path, the organ's.** Cues go to `JournalPath.retrieve`; nothing is duplicated on the model side, nothing scans; the journal is the organ of Slices A to E, sealed, policy `stop` during a measurement.
3. **The journal enters as content, through a zero init cross attention over the retrieved bytes, not as prompt tokens.** The read window's bytes are embedded with the model's own byte embeddings and attended by the residual stream in one declared block (`journal_block`, one hippocampal port). Journal OFF = the reader absent = N1 exactly; the two arms of the protocol differ only by that.
4. **The router decides the read, on the floor.** The reader's contribution is multiplied by the path 4 gate (`journal_gate`): pinned on or off for the protocol's arms (declared, recorded), or the router's path 4 weight, zero when the decision is another path or absent. A learned router on real spans stays a separate ablation (ADR-006 D9 held); the protocol does not claim it.
5. **The cue encoder is learned, its signal declared, leakage free.** A linear projection of the mean pooled residual stream at the journal block to a unit vector of the organ's dimension, trained by contrast (InfoNCE both ways: a query's cue must be nearest to its own statement's cue among the batch). One encoder for writing and reading; the DG, CA3, CA1 gate unchanged. Training facts come from the frozen protocol's generator family with disjoint seeds (100 000 and 100 001 against 0 and 10 000); every entity is checked against the protocol's facts and its negative control, a collision is removed and reported (`training_facts`).
6. **Organ use is taught, not assumed.** `cortex_c2b.organ_use` builds the curriculum: facts planted through the normal gated path under the current encoder (a memory scope, replanted every `journal_pool_refresh` steps), examples of a query and an answer under the contract, loss on the answer only, mixed with ordinary language modelling at `journal_curriculum_ratio`; every parameter is in the hashed configuration.
7. **Two processes, persistence declared.** Session A writes through the model's cues into the sealed journal; session B reopens the journal from the disk alone and the model from its checkpoint; the cues are reproduced because the encoder is in the checkpoint and CA3 is rebuilt from the log (the named boundary of ADR-007 D8, now a test on the model).
8. **A failure is attributable.** Next to the verdict: the retrieval hit rate (the encoder), the attention mass on the retrieved bytes (the reader), the attribute exactness given a valid citation (the answer). A FAIL says which of the three broke.
9. **Everything is a flag, a ledger record, a CPU smoke.** `journal: none | read` defaults to `none`; a run records `parent_run_id`, the architecture id `journal-…`, and the protocol's fields in `standard_suite`; `configs/smoke_journal_cpu.json` runs the whole loop in a minute on CPU and validates its record.

## Point (d), the abstention — decided as the combination of two levels

Three levels were laid out: no abstention (the model guesses; the negative control sits at chance and the run is valid, but the behaviour is a serene hallucination), a learned `<UNKNOWN>` token, and cite or abstain (the typed contract of RES-8 at the byte level: the answer carries the reference of the episode it used, verifiable by the harness; a claim without a valid citation is a hallucination even when the attribute is right by chance; the first real measurement of C4). The founder asked for the two levels combined so that the learning phase is mitigated, and the combination is one rule:

**The target of every training example is computed from what the journal actually returned, never from the ground truth alone.** If the episode holding the answer is among the retrieved ones, the target cites its label and gives the attribute; otherwise the target is `<UNKNOWN>`, even though the fact is in the journal. The model is never rewarded for citing what it was not shown, so no invented citation enters the signal; while the encoder is poor the model learns silence first (one token); as the contrastive loss improves retrieval, citations replace abstentions by themselves. The ramp is self paced.

**The contract.** `<EPI>` marks an episodic query; the answer is `<CITE>` + a one byte local label of a retrieved episode + `<ANS>` + the attribute + newline, or `<UNKNOWN>` + newline. The read window presents up to k episodes as `A:<payload>` lines in a seeded random order (a position is never a shortcut), each cut to its share of the byte budget. The harness maps the label back to the pointer and verifies that the cited episode holds the claimed attribute. Four of the eight reserved oracle tokens are taken (256 `<EPI>`, 257 `<CITE>`, 258 `<ANS>`, 259 `<UNKNOWN>`); the vocabulary does not change, so N1 loads as is; C3 keeps the other four.

**Three mitigations, declared and hashed.** An asymmetric weight on the decision token of an abstention target (answering instead of abstaining costs more than the reverse; default 2). Negative examples (never planted entities, a disjoint seed) from the first step. A bootstrap for the cold start: on a missed retrieval, with a probability that decays linearly to zero over the run, the right episode is forced into the window and the example is marked `forced`, counted in the diagnostics and never as recall.

**Committee amendments (2026-09-12), taken:** local labels instead of copying the 16 hexadecimal pointer in v1 (one byte to copy; the full pointer copy is an ablation); the decaying bootstrap; the invalid citation threshold declared before any run (1 %, as e_S). The false abstention needs no threshold of its own: it lowers the strict recall and is therefore already paid in the gap; it is reported as the cost of prudence.

**What the world does (checked 2026-09-12).** Kimi K2.6 is scored on a benchmark built to measure whether a model abstains rather than fabricates (65 % to 39 % hallucination across one iteration, method unpublished); OLMo 3's fully open trajectory shows that the vulnerability to fabricated statistics is present at SFT and persists through every later stage, the argument for a harness verified contract rather than a hope; Self RAG puts reflection and citation tokens in the vocabulary and labels them from evidence; Trust Align aligns to cite and to refuse and loses about ten points of its score when the unanswerable examples are removed, while prompting alone fails. Our rule is the same gesture as those labels, with the label taken from what the model was shown.

## What was built

`cortex_c2b/lm_bridge.py` (the cue encoder, the reader, the gate, the window, the contract, the bridge between the torch model and the numpy organ), `cortex_c2b/organ_use.py` (decontaminated facts, the pool, examples under the rule, the batch), `cortex_c2b/hm_lm.py` (the protocol's LM arm: both arms, the strict metrics, the attributable sub measures, the skill delta with the reader active on real spans, persistence and claimability), `train.py` (configuration, the reader in one block, the cue encoder in the model, the window forward with per token weights, the parent loader, the interleaved curriculum, the LM arm at the end into the record and as a file), `metrics/schema/run-v1.schema.json` (`training.parent_run_id`), `configs/smoke_journal_cpu.json` and `configs/kaggle_t4_journal.json` (step 4).

**Tests** (`tests/test_c2b_lm_bridge.py`, nine): the no op law and the strict parent loading; the window, the contract and its parser; the gate on the floor; the encoder learning by contrast with retrieval improving on a toy; the training facts disjoint from the protocol's; the target rule in its five cases and the loss weights; the LM arm's fields, the impossibility of a claim in memory and persistence across a sealed reopen; the model and its journal reopened in a new process with the same retrieval; the CPU smoke of the trainer with the journal on, its record validated (slow, run by CI).

**The CPU smoke told the truth on a toy** (two layers, 29 steps): the retrieval hit rose from 0.33 to 0.86 as the contrastive loss fell and the abstention target rate fell from 0.62 to 0.25, the self paced ramp in action; the protocol then reported a FAIL that is attributable: retrieval hit 0.6 (encoder half good), attention mass 0.93 (the reader looks at the bytes), false abstention 1.0 (silence first, the cold start the committee predicted, on 29 steps), invalid citation 0, negative control abstained 1.0, skill delta 0.07 (the reader active on random windows hurts a two layer toy). No number here is a result; it is the mechanics, working and honest.

## Reserves, recorded

Two forward passes per curriculum example (one partial, to the journal block, for the cue; one full with the window). The write gate rejects statements whose cues a poor encoder makes similar: early pools are smaller than planted, and the protocol's `admitted` count is reported for that reason. The skill arm measures the reader active on whatever the journal returns for ordinary spans, the honest e_S; a model that has not learned to ignore an irrelevant window pays it. The journal used for training is a memory scope; the run's own journal (the protocol's) is sealed on disk under `stop`. A learned router on real spans remains held. Nothing above is a claim about continuity: the first measurement is step 4.
